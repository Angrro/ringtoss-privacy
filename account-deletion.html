Delete from inside the app

The fastest way to delete everything is right in the game:

  1. Open Ring Toss.
  2. Tap Settings (bottom-right of the game screen).
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. Your account is removed immediately.

What gets deleted

  • Your anonymous device identifier.
  • Your chosen player name.
  • All game statistics (level, streaks, throws, hits, perfects).
  • Your entries on every leaderboard.

Deletion is permanent and takes effect right away. There is no account to recover afterwards; starting the game again creates a brand-new anonymous save.

Prefer to request it by email?

If you can no longer open the app, email nicolaeflorin100@gmail.com from any address and we will delete your data for you. We may ask for details that help us locate the right record. Requests are handled within 30 days.
