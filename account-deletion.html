Delete my data — Ring Toss

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Delete from inside the app (fastest)

Open Ring Toss and go to Settings → “Delete my data.” This permanently deletes your anonymous device ID, your display name, your game statistics, and all of your global leaderboard entries from our server. The action takes effect immediately and cannot be undone.

Can’t open the app?

Email nicolaeflorin100@gmail.com from the device you played on, with the subject “Delete my data.” We will delete your anonymous ID, display name, and all associated scores and leaderboard entries, and confirm by reply.

Ring Toss stores no email, password, phone number, or real name — only an anonymous random ID, an optional nickname, and gameplay statistics, all used solely for saving progress and the leaderboard. Data is hosted by Render (Frankfurt, EU).
