Ring Toss — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Ring Toss is a skill game. There is no sign-up, no login, no ads, and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. This policy explains the small amount of data the app can send to our server, and the fact that it is only sent with your consent.

Your consent comes first

On first launch the app asks whether you want to join the global leaderboard. Nothing is sent to our server — not your score, not your name, not even an identifier — until you tap “Accept.”

With your consent, your score and display name are uploaded to a global leaderboard. If instead you tap “Not now,” the game runs fully offline and nothing leaves your device. You can change this choice at any time in Settings → “Share my scores on the global leaderboard”; turning it off stops any further upload.

What we collect (only after you consent)

  • Anonymous device ID. A random identifier (a UUID) generated on your device the first time you enable sharing. It is not your Apple ID, not the advertising identifier (IDFA), and not a hardware ID. It exists only to link your saved progress to your leaderboard entry.
  • Display name. An optional, self-chosen nickname shown on the leaderboard. You can keep the default and never enter a real name.
  • Scores and game statistics. Your level reached, current and best streak, number of throws, hits, and perfect throws — used to save your progress and to build the global leaderboard.

Where your data is stored

Our server is hosted by Render in their Frankfurt, European Union region. Your anonymous ID, display name, and game statistics are stored there to power the leaderboard. We do not sell your data and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

What we do NOT collect

No email, password, or phone number. No real name. No contacts, photos, or precise location. No advertising identifier and no tracking across apps or websites. We do not use any advertising or analytics SDKs.

Deleting your data

You can permanently delete everything we hold at any time from inside the app: Settings → “Delete my data.” This removes your anonymous ID, nickname, game statistics, and all of your leaderboard entries from our server. If you cannot open the app, use the deletion page: Delete my data.

Children

Ring Toss is rated 4+ and contains no objectionable content, no chat, and no user-to-user messaging.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the text and the “Last updated” date on this page.
