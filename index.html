Ring Toss — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Privacy Policy Delete Your Account

Ring Toss is a single-player skill game. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to save your progress and show leaderboards. We do not run ads, we do not track you across apps or websites, and we never sell your data.

No ads No tracking No data selling No account sign-up

1.What we collect

  • Anonymous device identifier — a random ID generated on your device the first time you play. It is not your name, email, phone number, advertising ID, or any Apple/Google account. It only lets the game recognize your save on the server.
  • Player name — only if you choose to set one. It is shown on the leaderboard. You can leave the default or change it at any time.
  • Game statistics — level reached, current and best streak, total throws, hits, and perfect throws. These power your progress and the leaderboards.

2.What we do NOT collect

  • No email address, phone number, or real-world identity.
  • No location, contacts, photos, or microphone data.
  • No advertising identifiers and no third-party analytics or ad SDKs.
  • No cross-app or cross-site tracking.

3.How we use the data

  • To save and restore your progress across sessions.
  • To validate throws on the server so leaderboards are fair.
  • To display Level, Streak, and Perfects leaderboards.

That is the full extent of processing. There is no profiling, no advertising, and no automated decision-making beyond scoring your throws.

4.Storage and sharing

Data is stored on our own backend (hosted in the EU) and locally on your device. We do not share your data with third parties. We use no advertising or analytics partners. Data may be disclosed only if required by law.

5.Data retention and deletion

Your data is kept while you play. You can permanently delete everything at any time from inside the app: Settings → Delete account. This erases your progress and removes your leaderboard entries. See the Delete Your Account page for details, or email us and we will delete your data on request.

6.Children

Ring Toss does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The anonymous identifier and optional nickname are not personal data in the traditional sense, and the game contains no ads, chat, or external links to user-generated content.

7.Your rights

You can access, change, or delete your data at any time through the app. For any request or question, contact us at nicolaeflorin100@gmail.com.
